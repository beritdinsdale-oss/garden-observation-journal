# My Garden Observation Journal

Use this journal at the end of each course module to connect course ideas with a real place you can observe.

## Your journal is private

Your answers are not graded or submitted to Canvas. They are saved in the browser and device you are using.

## Each entry follows the same rhythm

1. **Observe** a garden, balcony, yard, community garden, or nearby green space.
2. **Notice** by selecting what you saw.
3. **Reflect** in your own words.
4. **Capture** an optional photo.
5. **Try next** by choosing something to keep watching.

## Protect your work

Use **Download backup** if you may change devices, use another browser, or clear browser data.

## Print or save a PDF

At the end of the course, select **Print journal**. In the print window, choose your printer or **Save as PDF**.
