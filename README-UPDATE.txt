This update changes only the Weather & Climate journal section.
All other journal sections are retained from the current repository.

Weather & Climate now aligns with the extreme-weather activity by:
- focusing observation on extreme heat, heavy precipitation, dry conditions/drought, freezing rain/ice, and seasonal timing;
- asking learners to identify the trend most relevant to their own garden;
- connecting garden observations to projected future changes;
- explicitly incorporating scientific certainty;
- ending with a concrete observation to keep watching.

No changes to app.js, index.html, or styles.css are required.
